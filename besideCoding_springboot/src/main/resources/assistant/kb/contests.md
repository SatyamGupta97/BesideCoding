# Contests

## Finding a contest

Open **Contest** (`/contest`). The page has two tables:

- **Active & Upcoming Contests**: contests that are running now or start later.
- **Past Contests**: contests whose end time has passed.

Each row shows the title, timing, number of participants and a link to the contest.

## Joining a contest

1. Open a contest from the list.
2. Click the join button. You must be signed in.
3. Joining registers your participation; the contest problem list then opens.
4. Inside the contest, work through the problems and submit your answers before the end time.
5. Ending the contest marks your participation as finished. **You cannot rejoin a contest you
   have already ended.**

## Contest pages

- **Problem list**: the problems chosen for this contest.
- **Problem detail**: statement and answer box for one problem.
- **Leaderboard**: ranking by contest submissions.
- **Solutions**: the contest solutions page, available after the contest ends.

## Rules and timing

- A contest is active between its start time and its end time; outside that window it is upcoming
  or past.
- During a live contest, hints and solutions for the contest problems are blocked everywhere in
  the app, including the Ask Beside assistant. This is enforced by the server, not just hidden in
  the interface.
- The same block applies if a contest problem is opened through the normal problem page.
- Once the contest ends, solutions and assistant hints become available again.

## After the contest

Review the solutions page and the leaderboard, and use the assistant to walk through the
approach for anything you missed.
