# Getting started on besideCoding

besideCoding is a practice site for coding interview questions. You solve problems on your
own, take part in timed contests, read and write discussions, and track your progress on your
profile.

## Creating an account

- Open the Sign Up page (`/signup`).
- Fill in username, e-mail address, password and confirm password, then submit.
- Alternatively use **Continue with Google** on the sign-in page; a Google account creates the
  account for you without a password.
- If the e-mail is already registered the site asks you to sign in instead.

## Signing in and out

- Open the Sign In page (`/signin`) and use your e-mail and password, or **Continue with Google**.
- You stay signed in between visits through a session cookie. Signed-in users are recognised on
  every page; pages such as problems, contests, discussions and the profile ask you to log in
  first.
- To sign out, open the profile menu in the top-right corner of the navbar and choose log out.

## Where things are

- **Problems** (`/problems`): the full problem set with filters and search.
- **Contest** (`/contest`): active, upcoming and past contests.
- **Discuss** (`/discuss`): community discussions.
- **Contribute** (`/contribute`): submit your own problem for review.
- **Profile**: your progress, statistics, submissions and edit button.
