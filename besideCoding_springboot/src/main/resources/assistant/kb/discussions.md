# Discussions

## Reading discussions

Open **Discuss** (`/discuss`) to see the community feed. Each entry shows the title, category,
author and when it was posted, and opens the full thread.

## Creating a discussion

1. Open **Discuss** and choose **Create Discussion** (`/create-discussion`).
2. Enter a title.
3. Pick a category: **Question**, **Article**, **Contest** or **Interview**.
4. Write the content and submit. You are returned to the discussion list.

## Good practice

- Use **Question** for doubts about a problem, **Article** for write-ups, **Contest** for contest
  announcements and recaps, and **Interview** for interview experiences.
- Avoid posting hints or solutions for problems that belong to a live contest.
