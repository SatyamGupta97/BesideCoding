# Profile and account

## Editing your profile

1. Open your profile from the navbar (or go to `/profile`).
2. Click **Edit Profile** in the left panel, under your name.
3. A modal opens with the editable fields: name, college or company, e-mail, bio, Google ID,
   LinkedIn ID, GitHub ID and portfolio link.
4. Change what you need and click **Submit**. The page reloads with the new details.

Only non-empty fields are updated, so clearing a field and submitting will not erase the old
value.

## Changing your profile picture

- In the same **Edit Profile** modal, hover the round avatar at the top.
- A pencil icon appears over the picture; click it and choose an image file.
- The new picture is uploaded to Cloudinary when you click **Submit**.
- Uploads are limited to 5 MB. Use a normal image format such as PNG or JPG.

## What your profile shows

- Your name, college or company, bio and avatar.
- Solved counts per difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard) as progress bars.
- Submissions grouped by language/category.
- Tabs for recent accepted submissions and your discussions.

## Passwords

There is no password-reset screen in the app yet. If you signed up with e-mail and password and
forgot it, sign in with Google using the same e-mail address, or ask an administrator for help.
